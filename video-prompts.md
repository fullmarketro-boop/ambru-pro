## 1. Master Prompt — Main Landing Background Video

### Prompt title
Ambru Scroll Hero — Control Room Atmosphere Loop

### Objective
Create the primary full-bleed background video for the first viewport and scroll-linked hero: a living environment that signals clarity, systems, and calm authority while leaving space for brand, headline, and CTA.

### Use case in the website
Main landing page scroll-driven background (hero → early sections). Loopable plate behind typography and CTA overlays.

### Main prompt
Slow cinematic photoreal video of an empty modern strategy “control room” matching Ambru brand stills: deep ink `#0B1F2A` and slate-blue `#1F3A4A` architectural planes, soft-mist `#E8EEF1` daylight drifting gently through a large frosted window, a long clean desk with a closed matte laptop, and a panoramic frosted-glass board with abstract non-readable system lines and nodes only. Extremely slow, stable motion — almost still. A thin signal-teal `#1FA6A0` reflection travels softly along the glass edge (subtle, never neon). Generous negative space across the left or right third for website overlays. Premium consultant atmosphere, sparse props, controlled contrast, no people, no logos, no readable text, no UI. Continuity with brand-hero-reference, brand-material-reference, and brand-workspace-reference materials and palette. Photoreal, polished, commercial website background plate.

### Negative prompt
people, faces, hands, logo, watermark, brand mark, third-party logo, readable text, letters, numbers, UI, dashboard, neon glow, purple gradient, cyberpunk, robot, hologram, HUD, chaotic camera, handheld shake, fast motion, whip pan, zoom punch, clutter, busy background, collage, glitch, heavy film grain, lens flare spam, smoke spectacle, cartoon, low resolution, warped perspective

### Continuity with reference images
Match the empty architectural language of the banner/hero environment: same ink walls, mist light, frosted glass board, matte laptop, sparse desk. Inherit material language from `brand-material-reference.png` (matte, frosted, brushed, fine paper feel) and workspace color temperature from `brand-workspace-reference.png` — without introducing people from those stills.

### Product / service consistency notes
Visual metaphor for Ambru Professional System: overview boards, process clarity, optimized workflows, AI-as-system (implied by calm structure), not gadget hype. Feels like a senior strategist’s room between sessions.

### Motion direction
Near-still living plate: slow daylight shift, soft particulate in light, gentle teal edge reflection drift, minimal ambient micro-movement on glass. No object animation that steals focus.

### Camera movement
Locked-off or imperceptibly slow push-in (≤3–5% scale over full duration). Level horizon. No tilt, no orbit, no parallax chaos.

### Lighting direction
Diffused natural soft-mist key from window; controlled interior fill; faint teal edge reflection only; gentle contrast; no dramatic gels.

### Materials / detail emphasis
Frosted glass board, matte ink walls, soft brushed metal laptop shell, clean desk surface — quiet premium textures readable even under overlay.

### Environment / atmosphere guidance
Empty, aligned, breathable control room. “I know where I am and what comes next.” Sober modern clarity.

### Composition guidance for headline and CTA overlays
Keep subject mass on one side; reserve a clean third (prefer left or right) with low detail for brand wordmark, one headline, one support line, CTA group. Avoid bright hotspots or moving teal in the overlay zone.

### Duration recommendation
8–12 seconds seamless loop (or 10s with matched first/last frame)

### Aspect ratio recommendation
16:9 (primary); also protect center for 9:16 crop safety if needed later

### Render quality recommendation
Photoreal 4K (3840×2160), high bitrate H.264/H.265, low noise, website-optimized master

### File naming recommendation
`brand-scroll-background.mp4`

## 5. Technical Adaptation Note — Scroll-Driven Websites

### Prompt title
Scroll Integration Notes (Ambru)

### Objective
Ensure any chosen render behaves as a website motion layer, not as a narrative ad that fights scroll and typography.

### Use case in the website
Implementation guidance for hero/background video tied to scroll position, sticky hero, or scrubbed timeline.

### Main prompt
N/A — technical note (not a generation prompt).

### Negative prompt
N/A

### Continuity with reference images
Poster frame / first frame should closely match `brand-hero-reference.png` environment language (or soft-mist alt) so image→video swap has no visual jump.

### Product / service consistency notes
Video supports brand promise (clarity / system / result) as atmosphere only; messaging stays in HTML text overlays, never burned into frames.

### Motion direction
Design for scroll:
- Prefer near-still loops under autoplay
- If scrub-on-scroll: map 0–100% scroll to a single slow push-in or light-breath curve (no scene cuts)
- Never require sound for comprehension

### Camera movement
Keep total transform subtle so parallax + scroll + overlay don’t create nausea. Cap combined motion.

### Lighting direction
Maintain stable mid-frame exposure for WCAG-friendly text contrast; avoid flicker and strobing highlights.

### Materials / detail emphasis
High-frequency detail (glass grain, board lines) should sit outside the primary text column to prevent shimmer under compression.

### Environment / atmosphere guidance
Loop must be seamless; first and last frames matched. No narrative beginning/middle/end.

### Composition guidance for headline and CTA overlays
- Safe overlay zone: ~40% of frame width, consistent side across breakpoints
- Keep faces/people out (per pack rules) so attention stays on copy
- Provide a static poster image (`brand-hero-reference.png`) for reduced-motion / autoplay-blocked states
- Mobile: prefer fallback plate or paused poster; reduce loop length and resolution
- Export: muted, `playsinline`, no captions burned in; real captions/copy in DOM

### Duration recommendation
Master/alts: 8–12s loop · Fallback: 6–8s · Scrub source may be same file with timeline mapped to scroll

### Aspect ratio recommendation
Master 16:9; keep subject continuity for center-crop to 4:5 / 9:16 if using full-bleed mobile

### Render quality recommendation
Deliver:
1. 4K master  
2. 1920×1080 web derivative  
3. Lightweight mobile derivative or static poster  
Target smooth 30fps (24fps acceptable if smoother temporally). Avoid aggressive bitrate that introduces macroblocking on ink flats.

### File naming recommendation
Primary: `brand-scroll-background.mp4`  
Alts: `brand-scroll-background-alt-a.mp4`, `brand-scroll-background-alt-b.mp4`  
Fallback: `brand-scroll-background-fallback.mp4`  
Poster still: `brand-hero-reference.png`
