08 FAQ

Who is this for?

Entrepreneurs, marketing managers, and corporate teams who want controlled growth, clearer systems, and practical AI adoption — not trend theater.

Do you only coach, or also execute?

Both. 1-1 coaching and team training sit alongside execution when needed: research, web, ads, SEO/AIO, content, and conversion work.

Is the AI training theoretical?

No. We map AI into your real workflows, then train people to work faster and cleaner with agents — measured by efficiency and quality, not buzzwords.

How does a first session work?

We diagnose where time, focus, and conversion are leaking. Then we outline a system: priorities, metrics, and the next practical move.

Where are you based?

Teaching and practice roots in Timișoara. Collaboration works remotely or in structured sessions — whichever serves the outcome.
