# 01-brand-kit-agent.md

When this prompt begins, ask the user only this single question before doing anything else:

"Do you already have a brand, or would you like me to create one from scratch?
A) I already have a brand
B) Create a brand from scratch"

---

If the user selects A (existing brand):
Ask the following questions to gather the brand details needed to build the brand kit:

1. What is your brand name and slogan?
2. How is your brand positioned in the market, and who is your target audience?
3. How would you describe your brand personality?
4. What is your color palette? Please share hex codes if available.
5. What typography or fonts does your brand use?
6. Do you have a logo or logo direction you can describe?
7. How would you describe the product or service itself — materials, features, key specs, differentiators?
8. What are the key benefits you want to highlight?
9. What is the visual mood of your brand — lighting style, textures, atmosphere?
10. What is the primary goal of the landing page you want to build?

Once all answers are collected, create a complete and practical brand kit and save it as:

brand-kit.md

Inside brand-kit.md, include:

1. Brand name
2. Brand slogan
3. Product or service name
4. Brand positioning
5. Target audience
6. Brand personality
7. Color palette with hex codes
8. Typography direction
9. Logo direction
10. Product or service description
11. Key benefits
12. Visual mood
13. Website goal
14. Suggested landing page sections
15. Image direction
16. Video direction
17. Website content direction

---

If the user selects B (create from scratch):
Ask only these questions before proceeding:

1. What type of product or service is this for?
2. What general visual style or mood do you want?
3. Is there anything specific you want the brand name, personality, or color direction to reflect?

Once those answers are collected, create a complete brand kit and save it as:

brand-kit.md

Use the user input to shape the brand system.

---

Important rules:
- Do not generate images.
- Do not generate videos.
- Do not build the website.
- Do not create subfolders.
- Keep all outputs in the current working folder.
- Only create the brand kit for now.
- Do not use real brand names or third-party logos unless the user explicitly provides their own brand assets.
