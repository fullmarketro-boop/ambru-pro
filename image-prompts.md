# Image Prompt Pack — ambru.pro

Source of truth: `brand-kit.md`  
Purpose: structured prompts for external image generation only. Do not generate images in this step.

**Global consistency lock**
- Brand: Ambru / ambru.pro — senior marketing, systems, and AI strategist
- Mood: calm, precise, professional “control room” clarity — not spectacle
- Palette: Deep Ink `#0B1F2A`, Slate Blue `#1F3A4A`, Signal Teal `#1FA6A0` (accent only ~10–15%), Soft Mist `#E8EEF1`, Warm Graphite `#2A2E32`, Fog Gray `#9AA6AD`, Pure White `#FFFFFF`, rare Copper Pulse `#C47A4A`
- Style: photoreal editorial, controlled contrast, generous negative space, strong alignment, few elements
- Avoid everywhere: logos, watermarks, readable UI text, typography baked into the image, purple gradients, neon AI kitsch, 3D robots, rocket/brain icons, generic handshakes, heavy grain, glitch, collage chaos, Inter/Roboto aesthetic cues

## 1. Hero Reference Image

### Prompt title
Ambru Hero — Authority Control Room Portrait

### Objective
Establish the brand’s first-viewport visual authority: a senior strategist presence inside a calm, structured professional environment that signals clarity, systems, and control.

### Use case in the website
Full-bleed hero background / dominant hero visual plane behind brand name, one headline, short support line, and CTA group.

### Main prompt
Photoreal editorial hero photograph of a confident Mid-European male professional in his mid-40s, smart-casual business attire in deep ink and warm graphite tones, standing with calm authority in a modern minimalist office “control room.” Soft mist daylight from a large window, deep ink and slate-blue architectural planes, a clean desk with a closed matte laptop and a blank process whiteboard with abstract non-readable diagram lines only. Sparse composition, generous negative space on one side for website overlay, strong vertical and horizontal alignment, premium consultant atmosphere, clarity and structure, subtle signal-teal accent only as a tiny desk object or soft edge reflection (max 10–15% of color presence). No logos, no readable text, no screens with UI, no neon, no purple. Shot as a commercial website hero still, full-bleed cinematic framing, photoreal, polished, controlled.

### Negative prompt
logo, watermark, brand mark, third-party logo, readable text, typography, letters, numbers, UI screens, dashboard text, neon glow, purple gradient, cyberpunk, robot, 3D AI brain, rocket icon, handshake stock photo, crowded desk, clutter, collage, glitch, heavy film grain, oversaturated colors, cartoon, illustration, low resolution, distorted face, extra fingers, fisheye, cheesy smile, influencer pose

### Brand consistency notes
Must feel ink + signal teal editorial-tech: Deep Ink dominance, Soft Mist light, teal only as restrained accent. Personality: competent, structured, calm mentor — never hype startup.

### Subject consistency notes
Same person archetype across pack if a figure appears: Mid-European man, mid-40s, short neat hair, clean shave or light stubble, composed expression, smart-casual (charcoal/ink blazer or fine knit over dark shirt). Not a stock “tech bro,” not overly formal tuxedo energy.

### Composition notes
One dominant subject + environment as supporting plane. Leave clean negative space (left or right third) for brand/headline overlay. Edge-to-edge hero crop. No floating cards, badges, or stickers in frame.

### Lighting notes
Diffused natural daylight key + soft controlled fill; gentle contrast; optional faint teal edge reflection only — never aggressive neon rim light.

### Materials and texture notes
Matte glass, soft brushed metal, fine digital paper feel on surfaces, subtle grid implied by architecture — no heavy grain, no glossy plastic tech clichés.

### Color usage notes
Base: `#0B1F2A`, `#1F3A4A`, `#E8EEF1`, `#2A2E32`. Accent: `#1FA6A0` sparingly. Avoid purple. Copper `#C47A4A` only if a tiny warm highlight is needed.

### Mood notes
“I know where I am and what comes next” — confidence, structure, sober modernity.

### Camera / lens notes
Full-frame, 35mm or 50mm, eye-level or slight low angle for quiet authority, shallow-to-moderate depth of field (subject sharp, background readable but soft), no Dutch tilt.

### Aspect ratio recommendation
16:9 (primary web hero) or 3:2

### Quality / realism recommendation
Photoreal commercial photography, high detail, natural skin texture, website-ready, 4K feel

### File naming recommendation
`brand-hero-reference.png`

## 2. Material / Detail Reference Image

### Prompt title
Ambru Material Detail — Systems Tools Still Life

### Objective
Show the tactile “system” language of the brand: tools of clarity, process, and measurement without product packaging or logos.

### Use case in the website
Materials / craft / process detail section; supporting visual near “Cum lucrezi” or benefits; texture break between long-copy sections.

### Main prompt
Photoreal extreme close-up still life of premium consulting work tools arranged with precise alignment on a soft-mist `#E8EEF1` desk surface: matte black notebook with blank pages, graphite pen, printed abstract process flowchart with non-readable geometric nodes and connecting lines only, frosted glass card, and a slim brushed-metal ruler. Deep ink shadows, slate-blue soft backdrop plane, one tiny signal-teal `#1FA6A0` accent object (a pin, clip, or edge of a marker) limited to a small color note. Macro detail of paper fiber, matte glass, soft brushed metal. Editorial product-detail aesthetic for a senior marketing and systems brand. No logos, no readable words, no numbers, no UI. Clean, sparse, highly controlled composition.

### Negative prompt
logo, watermark, readable text, letters, numbers, brand labels, sticky notes with writing, phone UI, laptop screen content, neon, purple, robot, AI hologram, clutter, messy desk, plastic junk, cartoon, illustration, heavy grain, glitch, busy patterns

### Brand consistency notes
Same ink/teal/mist system. Feels practical and structured — tools of diagnosis and process, not gadget porn.

### Subject consistency notes
Inanimate subject only. Same material language as other frames: matte, frosted, brushed, fine paper — no glossy consumer-tech candy colors.

### Composition notes
Tight crop, strong diagonal or ruled alignment, one clear focal cluster, ample calm negative space in one corner. No card UI overlays.

### Lighting notes
Soft side daylight, gentle specular on metal/glass, controlled shadows that read as depth not drama.

### Materials and texture notes
Fine paper tooth, matte ink surfaces, frosted glass, soft brushed metal; avoid heavy grain and plastic shine.

### Color usage notes
Soft Mist ground, Deep Ink / Warm Graphite tools, Signal Teal micro-accent only, Fog Gray soft shadow tones.

### Mood notes
Precision, craft, calm intelligence — “systems you can touch.”

### Camera / lens notes
Macro / 90–100mm equivalent, shallow depth of field, focus on paper edge or metal detail, tripod-steady commercial still.

### Aspect ratio recommendation

### Quality / realism recommendation

Behind them, a large whiteboard with abstract non-readable system map lines and nodes only, and a desk monitor showing a soft abstract slate-blue interface blur with no readable UI text. Soft mist daylight, deep ink wall planes, generous space, sparse props, subtle signal-teal accent in a single notebook band or mug rim.
